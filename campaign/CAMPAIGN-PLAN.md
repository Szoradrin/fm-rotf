# Rime of the Frostmaiden — Campaign Continuation Plan

**Status:** Active campaign — planning document
**Ruleset:** D&D 2024
**Source:** Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden
**Current Chapter:** 4 — Destruction's Light
**Immediate Location:** Easthaven
**Document Purpose:** Preserve campaign decisions and establish the planned progression through Sunblight Fortress, Bryn Shander, and the next major story arc.

## 1. Campaign Overview
The adventuring party is currently pursuing Xardorok Sunblight's chardalyn dragon as it attacks Ten-Towns.

The party has determined that Easthaven is the most likely location where they can intercept the dragon and is traveling there.

Three Ten-Towns settlements have already suffered devastating attacks. Their precise identities, casualties, and damage levels should be reconciled with the campaign's session history.

The party's immediate objective is to stop the dragon before it can devastate additional settlements.

### Campaign Direction
The planned sequence is:

1. Battle the chardalyn dragon in Easthaven.
2. Force the damaged dragon to retreat toward Sunblight Fortress.
3. Pursue the dragon back to Sunblight.
4. Clear Sunblight Fortress and permanently destroy the dragon.
5. Return to Bryn Shander as heroes.
6. Meet Vellynne Harpell and receive the next major quest.
7. End the current story arc with the dramatic appearance of Arveiaturace, the ancient white dragon.
8. Continue toward Auril's island, the Reghed Glacier, and Ythryn.

Important: The white dragon's appearance is intended as a major cliffhanger, not an immediate mandatory combat encounter.

## 2. Tonight's Session — Battle for Easthaven

### Starting Situation
The party is traveling toward Easthaven to intercept the chardalyn dragon.

The dragon has already devastated three settlements and is following its attack route through Ten-Towns.

Easthaven becomes the setting for a major defensive battle.

### Session Objectives
* Give the players a memorable battle against the chardalyn dragon.
* Showcase the dragon's destructive capabilities.
* Provide opportunities for tactical positioning, civilian rescues, and environmental interaction.
* Allow the party to inflict meaningful damage.
* Have the dragon retreat to Sunblight Fortress after suffering sufficient damage.
* Establish the party's next objective: pursuing and permanently destroying the construct.

### Encounter Structure

**Phase 1 — Dragon Arrival**

The dragon approaches Easthaven from the direction established by its previous attacks.

The party witnesses the threat to the town and must decide where to make its stand.

The encounter should establish the dragon's aerial mobility, radiant breath weapon, and ability to devastate buildings.

**Phase 2 — Battle Across Easthaven**

The dragon attacks the party and surrounding structures.

Potential tactical features include:

* Snow-covered streets and intersections
* Buildings providing cover
* Rooftops and elevated positions
* Frozen harbor and docks
* Barricades, wagons, and abandoned equipment
* Civilians requiring evacuation

The dragon should behave as a mobile siege weapon, not a stationary monster.

**Phase 3 — Dragon Retreat**

Once the party has inflicted sufficient damage, the dragon disengages.

Proposed homebrew trigger: At approximately 30% remaining hit points, the dragon initiates a retreat toward Sunblight Fortress.

The exact trigger should be finalized before the encounter.

The dragon flies directly toward Sunblight, crossing Lac Dinneshere and continuing toward the Spine of the World.

The party can observe its direction of travel.

**Phase 4 — Aftermath**

Easthaven survives the attack, although the extent of damage depends on the battle.

The party has driven off the immediate threat but has not destroyed it.

Survivors recognize that the dragon may return.

The players now have a clear reason to pursue it.

### Session Ending
The intended stopping point for tonight is the aftermath of the Easthaven battle and the dragon's retreat.

The party should understand that Sunblight Fortress is its likely destination.

The pursuit and fortress assault are reserved for the following session.

## 3. Easthaven Battle Map Strategy
The official Easthaven town map is the geographic reference.

Rather than creating a single enormous battle map, the encounter will use multiple tactical maps representing important locations.

### Planned Maps

**Map A — Frozen Harbor and Ferry**

* Frozen waterfront
* Docks and fishing vessels
* Warehouses and storage buildings
* Open terrain and limited cover
* Opportunities for civilian rescues

**Map B — Town Hall and Market Square**

* Central defensive position
* Intersecting streets
* Market stalls and wagons
* Rooftops and barricades
* Multiple routes for the dragon to approach

**Map C — Residential District**

* Dense wooden buildings
* Narrow streets and alleys
* Civilians sheltering indoors
* Collapsing structures and environmental hazards

**Map D — Eastway and Stables**

* Evacuation route
* Wagons and livestock
* Open approach suitable for aerial attacks
* Potential secondary defensive position

### Map Requirements
* True overhead tactical perspective
* Gridless
* High-resolution output
* Consistent winter lighting and environment
* Geography informed by the official Easthaven map
* No preplaced player characters or monsters
* Compatible with virtual tabletop use

Not every map must be used in the same encounter.

Priority: Prepare the Town Hall/Market Square and Harbor maps first.

## 4. Next Session — Return to Sunblight Fortress

### Starting Situation
The wounded chardalyn dragon has returned to Sunblight Fortress.

Surviving duergar are attempting to restore the construct.

The party pursues it into the mountains.

### Primary Objectives
1. Locate the dragon.
2. Infiltrate or assault Sunblight Fortress.
3. Interrupt repairs.
4. Permanently destroy the chardalyn dragon.
5. Defeat Xardorok Sunblight and remaining hostile forces, as applicable.
6. Investigate the fortress and recover useful information.

### Dragon Repair Mechanic — Homebrew
The dragon's return for repairs is a campaign modification.

Establish a visible countdown representing the duergar's efforts to repair the construct.

Potential mechanics:

* Repairs restore a limited amount of hit points.
* Sabotaging the forge interrupts progress.
* Killing or distracting repair crews delays repairs.
* Destroying critical equipment permanently disables repair capabilities.

The players should have meaningful opportunities to affect the outcome.

### Underdark Passage
Sunblight Fortress contains an Underdark connection.

The party may become curious about exploring it.

Design decision: Do not arbitrarily block the passage.

Potential future additions include:

* Abandoned duergar supply routes
* Chardalyn mining operations
* Myconid-related encounters
* Quaggoths and spore servants
* Evidence of illithid activity

These additions are optional and should not distract from the immediate dragon threat.

The exact published passage and any existing campaign encounters must be verified before designing this expansion.

## 5. Return to Bryn Shander
After destroying the dragon and resolving Sunblight Fortress, the party returns to Bryn Shander.

### Town Status
Bryn Shander remains intact.

The chardalyn dragon never reaches the town.

However, the settlement is dealing with the wider disaster:

* Refugees from devastated towns
* Supply shortages
* Fear of additional attacks
* Political tension among surviving town leaders
* Questions about the long-term survival of Ten-Towns

### Reception
The party receives a hero's welcome.

Their actions prevented the destruction of additional settlements and eliminated the immediate dragon threat.

The celebration should acknowledge both the party's victory and the suffering elsewhere.

### Character-Specific Hook
**Vistel — Emerald Enclave**

Vistel has an established Emerald Enclave contact.

The return to Bryn Shander provides an opportunity to reconnect with that contact.

Possible discussion topics:

* Environmental collapse caused by the Everlasting Rime
* Threats to wildlife and the natural balance
* Refugee and survival conditions
* The need to address Auril's magic directly

The contact's identity and prior interactions should be confirmed from campaign notes.

## 6. Vellynne Harpell's Introduction
Vellynne Harpell, a necromancer of the Arcane Brotherhood, approaches the party following its victory over the chardalyn dragon.

She recognizes that the adventurers have demonstrated the capability to confront threats beyond the abilities of ordinary Ten-Towners.

### Her Proposition
Destroying the dragon saved Ten-Towns from immediate destruction.

However, the Everlasting Rime continues.

Auril the Frostmaiden's magic is slowly destroying the region.

Vellynne believes the lost Netherese city of Ythryn contains magic capable of counteracting the endless winter.

### Key Information
* Auril is responsible for the Everlasting Rime.
* Ythryn lies buried beneath the Reghed Glacier.
* The city contains powerful ancient Netherese magic.
* The mythallar may provide a means to restore the region's climate.
* The Codicil of White is needed to open a passage through the glacier.
* The Codicil is located on Auril's island of Solstice.

### Vellynne's Role
Vellynne becomes an uneasy ally and expedition organizer.

She is intelligent, ambitious, pragmatic, and motivated partly by her own desire to discover Netherese secrets.

She should not be portrayed as an entirely benevolent mentor.

The party remains responsible for deciding whether to trust her.

## 7. Story Arc Cliffhanger — Arveiaturace

### Planned Event
Following the Sunblight victory and Vellynne's introduction, Arveiaturace, the ancient white dragon known as the White Wyrm, appears near Bryn Shander.

This is intended as a dramatic cliffhanger marking the end of the current campaign arc.

### Canonical Background
Arveiaturace is an ancient white dragon associated with Icewind Dale.

She carries the corpse of her former wizard companion, Meltharond, upon her back.

She is extremely dangerous.

The published adventure does not require her to attack Bryn Shander.

### Campaign Treatment
The dragon's appearance should communicate overwhelming power and uncertainty.

Possible presentation:

* An unnatural silence settles over the town.
* A massive shadow passes across the rooftops.
* An ancient white dragon emerges from the storm.
* A motionless wizard sits upon her back.
* The dragon circles Bryn Shander and notices the adventurers.

End of session / story arc.

### Important Constraints
* Do not require an immediate fight.
* Do not assume the party can defeat an ancient white dragon.
* Do not predetermine the dragon's ultimate purpose.
* Develop her motivations and subsequent interactions during future planning.

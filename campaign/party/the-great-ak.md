## Personality/Story
- Neutral Good, Male
- **Ideal:** "I speak for the Trees because they have no voice!" Demands respect — "I am the Great Ak, if you please." Believes himself the Master Woodsman of the World, ruler of the mystical Forest of Burzee. Guides young Claus through the world of mortal men to witness human sorrow and hardship, inspiring Claus to make toys for poor children. (L. Frank Baum mythology reference — this is the character's self-mythologized identity, played straight at the table.)
- **Flaws:** Can get angry when supporting the Forest and Nature — may charge when he feels strongly about it.
- **Personality quirks:** Can be big and clumsy, might knock things over accidentally. Refers to himself as "AK" in third person / targets things with "AK Quired." Puns constantly on his name — "AXE him a question," "let me AXE you a question." Considers almost everyone an "Un-Natural Creature." **Believes the Frostmaidens are an abomination and perversion of nature.** Not interested in towns or staying in a stable — a group of trees is shelter enough. Explicitly noted: doesn't have combat tactics (charges in on instinct/emotion).
- Lore (in-sheet, real-world moose biology flavor text): 7 ft tall, 5 ft antler span, 1200 lbs, huge appetite, can go without food for some time.

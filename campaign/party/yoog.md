## Personality/Story
- Neutral Good, Male, Red/Brown eyes, 7'2", 360 lbs
- **Backstory:** The Cragshadow Clan of Bugbears and the Bloodfrost Yeti Clan reluctantly ended a long territorial war by exchanging infant sons to be raised by their former enemies. Yoog, son of the Cragshadow chief, was raised among yetis. His upbringing was rough — the yetis' bloodthirsty cruelty compounded his misery. He preferred to hunt for pets to love, but they were often crushed under his overbearing hugs. He kept trying to find a special "aminal" that would survive.
- Ran away from the Bloodfrost Clan on reaching adulthood, seeking friends who wouldn't abuse him like the yetis did — and hoping to find a furry "aminal" that could be his forever pet and friend.
- No ideals/bonds/flaws filled in on the sheet as of last capture.

## DM Notes
- Genuinely warm, sympathetic backstory underneath a big scary frontline warrior — raised as a peace hostage among his own people's enemies, just wants a pet that survives his hugs. Strong hook for any wildlife/companion-adjacent story beat — he'd likely be protective or emotionally invested if the party encounters something like an injured or orphaned animal.
- Highest raw HP in the party (90) and strong burst damage via Rage + Reckless Attack + Polearm Master — a real frontline tank/striker hybrid.

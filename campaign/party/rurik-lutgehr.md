## Personality/Story
- **Ideal:** People. I'm loyal to my friends, not to any ideals, and everyone else can take a trip down the Styx for all I care. (Neutral)
- **Bond:** Something important was taken from me, and I aim to steal it back.
- **Flaw:** If there's a plan, I'll forget it. If I don't forget it, I'll ignore it.
- Always calm, never raises his voice or lets emotions control him. Incredibly slow to trust.
- Faith: Vergadain, god of luck and wealth
- **Background — Criminal Contact (Spy):** Reliable, trustworthy contact who acts as a liaison to a network of other criminals; knows how to get messages to/from his contact over great distances (local messengers, corrupt caravan masters, seedy sailors)
- **Backstory:** Informant to Icewind Dale dwarves, keeping them updated on the area so they can stay focused on mining. Travels between Ten-Towns and Luskan, guards caravans, performs at taverns/inns. Plays cards and gathers gossip when not performing. As a performer, sometimes gets special access to areas and can gather additional info.
- **Personal quest — Ring Hunter:** Looking for the "knucklehead trout" that bit off his father's finger, which had a signet ring on it. Must retrieve the lost ring to preserve his inheritance.

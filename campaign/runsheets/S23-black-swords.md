## Arrival (no travel scene)

- Gate (C1): guards refuse — "too sick to entertain guests" / "no need for your heathen rituals" — **until Levistus's order lands.** The party already satisfies the alliance condition (they meddled with Nildar Sunblight in *The Unseen*). **[DM CALL]** Timing: the order arrives while they're at the gate (clean), or after they try another way in (more tension).

## The alliance — what it gives and what it costs
- Allied Knights do everything to make them safe and happy — **except give up the keep or Crannoc.** NE guard tower becomes the party's base.
- **Tripwires:** attacking any Knight except Kadroth; harming or hindering Avarice; abandoning the fight against the duergar.

### THE decision: alliance vs. rescue — Crannoc (C14) is guarded by Yajath
| Path | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stay allied, leave Crannoc | Base of operations; Crannoc stays a prisoner; no speaker map (see Hook). |
| Attack Yajath / rout the cult | Alliance ends; Crannoc freed; he calls the militia, offers them the castle forever, and can give the Speaker's Quest + map. |
| Get Yajath out of the way without attacking him | Charm, bribe, sneak, or Mere/Alassar distractions. **[DM CALL]** whether that counts as an attack. |

## Hethyl (C13) — the scene that matters
"I've been dreading this moment." Blunt, uncaring, already knows how this ends. She delivers four prophecies, then **dies of natural causes on the spot:**
1. Xardorok Sunblight will conquer Icewind Dale unless *they* defeat him.
2. His fortress is hidden on the surface in the Spine of the World; its forge runs on the still-beating heart of a red dragon.
3. His plan to destroy Ten-Towns can be found by searching the fortress.
4. They'll die if they face him too soon — they must test themselves against the horrors of the far reaches first.

**[DM CALL] on #4** (cuts against going straight to Sunblight): the book only asks for level 4+ and they're 6. Options: (a) she looks them over and adds "...you already have" — frost giants, the Elven Tomb, Sephek; (b) leave it ominous and let them choose; (c) she dies mid-sentence before finishing it.
**She gives no map.**

## Hook → Sunblight (ch. 3 Speaker's Quest)
A speaker (Crannoc, once free) says: duergar walk invisibly among us, hunting chardalyn to build a construct that will lay waste to Ten-Towns; the militia captured one and forced out the fortress location → **map to Xardorok's fortress.** The captured duergar was executed afterward (body in a cellar — speak with dead confirms). Reward: hospitality and friendship only.
**[ADAPT]** If they stay allied (Crannoc not freed), another route to the map: the book also puts one aboard the Easthaven ferry, or they can compel a captured duergar. **Six duergar are hiding in Dinev's Rest** at the south end of town (book: they stay hidden until ch. 4). **[DM CALL]** whether to let the party find them tonight — it's a clean way to get a live prisoner.

## Avarice (C21) — not a fight unless they make it one
"Get out." — then, recognizing them, as hospitable as she can manage; won't say why she's here. Leaves if she stops feeling safe. **Harm her → alliance breaks AND Gargle & Gurgle hit the party as they leave the keep.** Rebuilt actor is CR 7: Staff of Frost (cone of cold, ice storm, wall of ice), Banishing Rebuke (1/LR reaction), fireball, counterspell.
